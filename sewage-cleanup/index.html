Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. When it happens in Lake Elsinore, Lake Elsinore Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home

How to Tell You Need Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
