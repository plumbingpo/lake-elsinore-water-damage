Restoration Challenges Riverside Homeowners Face

Water trouble is not all equally urgent, and knowing the difference helps you decide what to do first. The five below are ones we see constantly in Riverside, listed from most pressing to least.

Bursts: Nothing is more time-critical than a broken water line, which keeps pouring until the supply is cut. Depending on the season, the break may follow a freeze, and it often shows only when the ice inside melts. Shut the house supply, run a tap to drain the lines and phone us immediately, whatever the hour. If a ceiling is sagging from the weight of water, stay out from under it and do not poke it.

Runoff: Rain running into the house at ground level ranks a close second. You cannot stop the rain, but you can move belongings up and keep away from any outlets near the water. Having passed over pavement and soil on the way in, it carries dirt and germs. As soon as it is safe to enter, we pump the water out and take away porous items it ruined.

Kitchens: A dishwasher that leaks from its door or a refrigerator line that drips behind the unit calls for same-day attention. Close the small valve under the sink or behind the appliance and the flow usually stops. What remains is the water trapped below the cabinets and floor covering, and it will not dry by itself. For Riverside kitchen jobs, our crew can frequently save the cabinets by pushing dry air in at the toe kick.

Crawlspaces: Dampness in the space below the floor can wait a few days, though not a few months. Puddles on the ground cover, dripping ductwork or wet insulation between the joists are what an inspection usually turns up. Given time, that humidity works its way upstairs and the joists start to rot. Our crew clears any standing water, brings the framing down to a normal moisture level and reports the likely cause.

Odors: A musty smell near the site of an old leak is the least urgent item here, yet it deserves an inspection soon. Since growth can start 24 to 48 hours after materials get wet, an old leak that was never fully dried is a likely culprit. Avoid wiping or bleaching a big area on your own, as that sends spores into other rooms. We check for hidden dampness, set up containment and clear out the affected material for homeowners in Riverside.

Preventing Water Damage in Riverside Homes

Most of the water damage we see in Riverside homes started with something small that gave warning first. A few minutes of checking a couple of times a year catches most of it.

Start with supply lines. The rubber hoses on washing machines, dishwashers, toilets and ice makers wear out, and braided stainless replacements are inexpensive. Look under every sink for stains, swollen cabinet floors or a musty smell. At the water heater, watch for corrosion or moisture around the bottom, and flush some sediment out annually. Outside, keep gutters clear and make sure downspouts send water away from the foundation.

Know where your main shutoff valve is and make sure everyone in the house can turn it. Leak sensors placed by the water heater, washer and under sinks cost little and will alert you early. If you find something in your Riverside home that worries you, such as a stain that keeps growing, have it looked at before it becomes a cleanup.
